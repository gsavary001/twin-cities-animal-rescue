Every Animal Deserves a Loving Home

Our Mission

Twin Cities Animal Rescue rescues, cares for, and rehomes dogs, cats, and small animals across our community. We believe that with a little help from caring neighbors, every pet can have a safe and happy life.

Our Impact

Thanks to adopters, fosters, volunteers, and donors, here is what we have done together:

  • 1,200+ animals adopted into loving homes
  • 350 active foster families
  • 600 volunteers giving their time
  • 15 community partners working with us
