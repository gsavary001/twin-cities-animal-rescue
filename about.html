About Twin Cities Animal Rescue

Our Story

Twin Cities Animal Rescue began when a small group of neighbors started taking in stray animals. Today we are a community nonprofit that works with volunteers, foster families, and local partners to give animals a second chance.

A Welcome Message

Core Values

  • Compassion: We treat every animal and every person with kindness.
  • Trust: We are open about how we care for animals and use donations.
  • Action: We turn caring into results, one adoption at a time.
  • Community: We work best when neighbors help neighbors.

Partner Acknowledgments

We thank our community partners for their support:

  • Twin Cities Veterinary Clinic, for low-cost medical care
  • Neighborhood Pet Supply, for food and supply donations
  • Local Public Library, for hosting adoption events
  • Community Volunteer Center, for connecting us with volunteers
