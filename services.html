Adoption, Foster, and Volunteer Services

Adoption Process

Adopting a pet is a happy day, and we want it to go smoothly. Here is how it works:

  1. Browse our available animals and choose one you would like to meet.
  2. Fill out a short adoption application.
  3. Meet your new pet with our adoption counselor.
  4. Sign the adoption agreement and pay the adoption fee.
  5. Take your new family member home.

Foster Program

Foster families give animals a safe place to heal and grow before adoption. We provide food, supplies, and veterinary care. You provide love and a temporary home.

  • Short-term fosters: 2 to 4 weeks
  • Long-term fosters: 2 months or more, for pets with special needs
  • Training and support from our foster coordinator
